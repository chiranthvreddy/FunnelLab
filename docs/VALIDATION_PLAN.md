# Research and usability validation plan

**Status:** Proposed only. No participant interviews or usability tests have been conducted.

## Recruiting
Recruit 5–7 small-team coordinators or founders who have tried a freemium collaboration tool. Avoid leading participants toward the proposed mobile hypothesis.

## Interview prompts
- Tell me about the last time you set up a workspace for your team.
- Where did you get stuck? What did you do instead?
- Which device did you use and why?
- What would make you invite a teammate or upgrade?
- What reports would help you diagnose onboarding?

## Usability tasks for FunnelLab
1. Identify the largest relative funnel drop-off and explain its denominator.
2. Compare mobile and desktop activation without claiming causation.
3. Explain week-1 retention and its cohort anchor.
4. Interpret a confidence interval spanning zero.
5. Modify an opportunity's RICE assumptions and export a decision memo.

## Capture
Task success, time on task, errors, confidence, confusion, quotes and feature requests. Obtain informed consent; anonymize notes.

## Decision thresholds (proposed, not achieved)
At least 4/5 complete the funnel task without help; 4/5 correctly explain retention anchor; 4/5 recognize an inconclusive experiment. Revisit the information architecture if thresholds fail.

## Ethics
Never present simulated personas or fabricated quotes as research findings. Do not collect personal data without consent.
