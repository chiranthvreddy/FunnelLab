# Fictional product scenario — TeamNest

FunnelLab analyzes a hypothetical freemium team collaboration product, **TeamNest** (fictional example, not a real customer).

**User:** Small-team project coordinator looking to organize tasks without an enterprise rollout.

**Core value:** Creating a shared workspace and inviting a teammate.

**Simplified synthetic event mapping:**
- Visit: unique visitor reaches landing page.
- Signup: visitor creates a trial account.
- Activate: account completes an illustrative first-value milestone. The synthetic generator does not record actual workspace or invite events, so these are proposed future tracking definitions, not observed behavior.
- Purchase: user upgrades to paid plan.

**Key question:** Is mobile onboarding friction reducing activation, and would simplifying it improve conversion without compromising quality?

**Counter-hypotheses:** Different mobile traffic intent, channel mix, lower purchase readiness, or instrumentation differences.

**Real discovery plan:** Interview five target coordinators, observe onboarding, capture failures and reasons, then instrument true workspace-creation and invitation events. None has been performed.

**Decision:** Prioritize investigation of the mobile onboarding hypothesis, not an unconditional product launch. A simulated experiment is provided to practice reading uncertain outcomes.

**North Star proposal:** Weekly teams creating and sharing at least one useful workspace. Current demo only measures a simplified activation proxy.
